# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technical, and constructive. Assume good faith, criticize ideas rather than people, and make room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving actionable review feedback;
- explaining trade-offs and evidence;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- keeping discussions relevant to the project;
- respecting privacy and avoiding the publication of credentials or personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, sexualized content directed at other participants, or repeated bad-faith behavior after a maintainer warning.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the project community. Enforcement decisions should be proportional to the behavior and may consider repeated patterns.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the maintainer privately through a channel listed on their GitHub profile. For software vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
